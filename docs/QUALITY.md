## Reshape assessment

**USTB is the strongest reshape, and it works.** The Chainlink reference directory (`reference-data-directory.vercel.app/feeds-mainnet.json`, SHA-256 `00566b28…`) lists "USTB NAV per Share": proxy `0x289B5036cd942e619E1Ee48670F98d214E745AAC`, aggregator `0xd5BC4e3c7e77A5776fD9D0DDe8471B8B4aEc10f5`, 6 decimals, heartbeat 95,400 s, porSource "Asset Manager API", porAuditor "Superstate". The reviewer walked phase-2 rounds back from Ethereum block 26,503,879 through PublicNode, about 435 `eth_call`s. That gave 430 rounds from 2025-09-27 to 2026-11-26, joined to the full `api.superstate.com/v1/funds/1/nav-daily` response (1,060 rows).

Reproduce these preliminary findings from pinned inputs before citing them as results.

- **Exact fidelity.** 0 of 430 feed answers lack an identical issuer value, and 288 of 288 distinct issuer NAVs since 2025-09-29 reached the feed. No issuer value needs more than 6 decimals.
- **Timing.** A NAV dated D reaches the feed on the next business day at about 13:15 UTC (EDT) or 14:15 UTC (EST), around 09:15 ET. Measured from 00:00 UTC of D, the lag has a median of 37.8 h, a 90th percentile of 86.2 h and a maximum of 110.2 h. Friday NAVs arrive Monday. Value changes happen only Monday to Friday. Weekend rounds are heartbeat refreshes 26.5 h apart. The longest gap was 95,484 s, 84 s over the heartbeat, on 2025-10-12/13.
- **Weekend and month-end example.** The 2026-09-30 NAV of 11.229908 posted at 2026-10-01 13:17:35 UTC. Over Oct 3–4 the issuer API labels carry Friday's 11.232233, while the feed holds Thursday's 11.231053 until Monday 13:15 UTC, about 1.05 bp behind the label.
- **Holiday.** On 2025-11-11 (Veterans Day) the feed still showed the 2025-11-07 NAV while the issuer labelled 2025-11-10 and 2025-11-11 at 10.888154. That puts the feed 3.11 bp behind, the largest gap in the window. Across all updates, the median deviation from the previous calendar day's issuer label is 0 bp and the 90th percentile is 1.01 bp.
- **Red-team limits to state next to the claims.**
  - The feed's source is the asset manager's API, so exact agreement is expected. The finding is about delivery timing and staleness, not independent valuation.
  - Issuer publication time is unknown, so "NAV date to onchain" combines NAV strike, issuer publication and oracle posting. It is an upper bound on oracle latency.
  - Weekend and holiday issuer labels are carry-forwards, not revaluations.
  - Phase 1, the Aave LlamaGuard wrapper (`0xde49c7B5…4Aa0`, heartbeat 97,200 s) and any RedStone or Chronicle USTB feeds were not read. The builder should either add them or say they were not read.

**BENJI is not a feed comparison.** No BENJI or Franklin feed appears in the Chainlink mainnet directory, and the filed NAV is 1.0000 on all 43 days. Use it only as a filing-lag control: the valuation date compared with EDGAR acceptance about six days after month-end.

**USDY is comparable in units, but this is unverified.** Ondo's onchain oracle (USD/token) can be compared with the LLC Section C Reference Token principal (USD/token). Ondo's docs say ContinuousPriceOracle took over on Ethereum on 2026-11-01 and that only the old oracle supports `getPriceHistorical`. The reviewer could not resolve the addresses: the docs address URLs tried returned 404. Treat this as an optional same-issuer fidelity check, not a third independent fund.

**OUSG lineage is a cheap upgrade.** One day already agrees to 1.4e-5 bp. A run of 15 to 20 daily statements would either establish that the chart is derived from administrator returns or refute it.
