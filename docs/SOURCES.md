## Business-day references

The model transcribes the exchange holidays within scope from the official [NYSE 2024](https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/ICE_NYSE_2024_Yearly_Trading_Calendar.pdf), [NYSE 2025](https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/ICE_NYSE_2025_Yearly_Trading_Calendar.pdf) and [NYSE 2026](https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/ICE_NYSE_2026_Yearly_Trading_Calendar.pdf) calendars, plus the [January 9, 2025 special closure](https://beta.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/markets/american-options/rule-interpretations/2025/National_Day_of_Mourning_20250102.pdf). Federal holiday labels use the [OPM scheduled calendar](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/). [SIFMA’s U.S. holiday archive](https://www.sifma.org/resources/general/us-holiday-archive) lists the 2024 and 2025 Columbus and Veterans Day closures. SIFMA also confirms the 2026 [Columbus Day](https://www.sifma.org/news/press-releases/sifma-fixed-income-market-close-recommendations-in-the-u-s-the-u-k-and-japan-for-u-s-columbus-day-and-japan-health-and-sports-day-holidays-2026) and [Veterans Day](https://www.sifma.org/news/press-releases/sifma-fixed-income-market-close-recommendation-in-the-u-s-the-u-k-and-japan-for-the-u-s-veterans-day-holiday-2026) closures. Its [January 9, 2025 notice](https://www.sifma.org/news/press-releases/sifma-recommends-early-market-close-on-january-9-2025-for-the-national-day-of-mourning-in-honor-of-former-president-carter) recommends closing fixed-income cash markets early. These are comparison calendars and recommendations. The issuer API does not expose a valuation-strike calendar.
