## Typical timing

Chainlink posted 588 of 601 Superstate USTB NAVs on the next date the issuer’s labelled NAV changed. Eight values never appeared in the Ethereum feed through the study cutoff in November 2026. Seven of those eight were from 2024.

[Delivery counts and unseen values](https://github.com/RahilBhavan/rwa-nav-feed-audit/blob/main/docs/RESULTS.md#full-history-and-coverage)

## The Friday NAV

Superstate’s first Friday USTB NAV of October 2026 reached Chainlink’s Ethereum feed Monday at 9:15 a.m. New York time. Monday was also the next date the issuer’s labelled NAV changed.

[Weekend comparison](https://github.com/RahilBhavan/rwa-nav-feed-audit/blob/main/docs/RESULTS.md#daily-gaps)

## Veterans Day

Superstate’s USTB series carried Monday’s NAV through Veterans Day 2025. Chainlink posted Monday’s value on Wednesday, the next date Superstate’s labelled NAV changed.

[Holiday comparison](https://github.com/RahilBhavan/rwa-nav-feed-audit/blob/main/docs/RESULTS.md#daily-gaps)

## September month-end

Superstate’s Sept. 30, 2026 USTB NAV appeared on Ethereum at 9:17 a.m. New York time the next morning. That’s the feed’s timestamp. Superstate’s NAV history gives a date label without a publication time.

[Month-end comparison](https://github.com/RahilBhavan/rwa-nav-feed-audit/blob/main/docs/RESULTS.md#month-end)
